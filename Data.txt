Data
The dataset (healthcare_dataset.csv) consists of 55,500 hospital admissions between May 2019 and May 2024. Each record has a patient name, age, gender, blood type, medical condition, admission and discharge dates, doctor, hospital, insurance provider, billing amount, room number, admission type, medication and test result. There are no missing values. The Medical Condition column is the target label.
During pre-processing, patient names are converted to Title Case, exact duplicates and near-duplicates (same patient, date, condition and bill with a slightly different age) are removed, and negative billing amounts are deleted. Two features are derived: length of stay (discharge date minus admission date) and admission month. The six categorical features are one-hot encoded and the four numeric features are standardised. The cleaned data is then split 80/20 by patient into 39,996 training and 9,908 test records (random_state = 42). The test records are saved to test_split.csv so that evaluation always uses data the model never saw during training.
