Data
The AI system will require relevant and reliable healthcare data to train, test, and operate the machine learning model. The data will include patient data such as patient ID, age, medical history, allergies, and genetic information. Clinical data will include symptoms, diagnoses, laboratory results, vital signs, and other clinical observations. This information will provide the model with the patient's medical background needed to identify relevant patterns.
The system will also require treatment data, including medications, previous treatments, dosages, drug reactions, and treatment outcomes. Historical medical data will include previous diagnoses, complications, hospital visits, allergies, and adverse medical events. This historical information is important because it allows the machine learning model to learn from previous patient cases and identify patterns that may indicate possible risks.
The system will also use research data, including medical studies, clinical guidelines, clinical trials, and drug information. This information will support the AI assistant when providing healthcare professionals with relevant medical information. Outcome data such as successful treatments, unsuccessful treatments, complications, adverse reactions, and patient outcomes will also be required. These outcomes provide labelled information that can be used for supervised machine learning.
Before the data is used, it will go through data preprocessing. Missing values will be identified and handled, duplicate or incorrect records will be removed, and categorical data will be converted into a suitable format for machine learning. Relevant features will then be selected, and the dataset will be divided into training and testing data. Appropriate privacy and security measures will also be applied because healthcare data contains sensitive patient information.
