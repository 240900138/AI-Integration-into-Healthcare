Machine Learning Approach
The proposed system will use Supervised Machine Learning because the model will be trained using historical patient data with known diagnoses, treatments, and outcomes. The main purpose of the machine learning approach is to identify patterns in patient information and predict possible medical risks, complications, or treatment-related outcomes. The system will support healthcare professionals by providing relevant information when reviewing a patient's medical history.
The first step will be data collection, where relevant historical patient records will be collected, including medical history, symptoms, laboratory results, diagnoses, medications, and treatment outcomes. The data will then be preprocessed by handling missing values, removing duplicate or incorrect records, and converting categorical information into a suitable format. Feature selection will then be performed to identify the most relevant patient information that can contribute to identifying medical risks and treatment outcomes.
The dataset will be divided into training and testing data. The training data will be used to teach the machine learning model, while the testing data will be used to evaluate how well the model performs on previously unseen information. Classification algorithms such as Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Logistic Regression will be considered because the system needs to classify possible medical outcomes. The selected model will be trained using the prepared historical patient data.
The model will be evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall and a confusion matrix to determine how accurately it identifies possible outcomes. If necessary, the model can be improved by adjusting its parameters and selecting more relevant features. Once the model has been evaluated, it can analyse new patient information and identify possible risks or outcomes based on patterns learned from historical data.
The trained machine learning model will form part of the AI healthcare assistant and provide supporting information to healthcare professionals. The system will help highlight possible risks and treatment-related information, while the final clinical decision will remain with the qualified healthcare professional.
