AI Solution

AI Integration Into Healthcare  works like a smart assistant built directly into the hospital's computer system, reading through a patient's entire medical record—including past lab results, doctor notes, and genetic details. It instantly compares this history against global medical research, clinical trials, and drug databases to catch things a human might miss. Instead of just showing standard treatments, it organizes results into simple lists that highlight normal choices alongside rare, less than 1% chance risks or alternative options, complete with links to the medical studies behind them. This gives doctors and students a clear, proactive warning system to help them make safer decisions and prevent unexpected complications before they happen.
