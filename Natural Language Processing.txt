Natural Language Processing
 Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a sub-field of Artificial Intelligence that allows computers to understand, interpret and process human language. The proposed AI Integration into Healthcare system will utilize NLP to examine the patients’ medical records, symptoms, complaints, medical histories and treatment notes. The system will identify critical medical information and transform unstructured health care information into structured data. This information can then be used by the AI model to detect trends and assist healthcare professionals.

The NLP process can involve text collection, text cleansing, tokenization, entity recognition, information retrieval, classification. The system can use Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) to convert the medical text into number features for use in machine learning classification. More sophisticated ones might use word embeddings or transformer based models to better understand medical terms. This can facilitate the analysis of large amounts of healthcare information and help reduce manual processing.
